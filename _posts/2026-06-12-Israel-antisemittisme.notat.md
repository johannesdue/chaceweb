In September 2025, in response to public debate in Norwegian media regarding the relationship between anti-Israel and anti-Jewish attitudes, I put together a brief analytical note based on publicly available data from two surveys of the Norwegian adult population. The data indicates that hostile attitudes toward Israel are robustly associated with antisemitic attitudes, and this relationship is relatively strong. The note (in Norwegian) can be accessed [here](https://johannesdue.github.io/israel-antisemittisme-notat/).
